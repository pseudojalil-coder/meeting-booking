Volunteer with us

Give a few hours a week and make a real difference to someone nearby.

Why volunteer?

Our volunteers cook, drive, call and visit. You choose what suits your week, and we train you for it.

Most roles need two to four hours a week. Some are weekday only, others run on weekends. Everyone starts with a short information session.

Roles we need right now

Meal delivery drivers · Friendly phone callers · Paperwork helpers · Weekend kitchen crew

How it works

  1. Book an information session
    Pick a time below. It's about 45 minutes, online or at our office.
  2. Check and training
    We'll help you with a police check and a short induction.
  3. Start helping
    We match you with a role and a buddy for your first few weeks.

Book an information session

Choose a time that suits you. You'll get an email with the details and a link to change it.

Questions

Do I need any experience?

No. We train you for every role.

Can I volunteer with a friend?

Yes, book a session each and tell us in the notes.

What if I can't make my session?

Use the link in your confirmation email to pick a new time.
